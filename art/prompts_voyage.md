# Part 2: sheep, pigs, sailors, islands

In the game the sheep, pigs and sailors come in many combinations (a sheep with or without a bell, a pig with a scarf in any of 6 colours, a sailor with a hat in any of 6 colours).
So the pictures are made **without** those extras, and the game adds them on top: the bell, the scarf and the hats get their colour from the game.
All animals face **right** (the game turns them around).
