# Nano Banana prompts: ground and platforms (quality pass)

Save in `art\raw\` with these names. Same style as everything else (stylized 2D cartoon, bold clean dark outlines, cel shading, rich colors).

The ground pictures use a **magenta** background (#FF00FF), not green, because grass is green and would be cut out with it.

Most important first: section 1 (Olympus ground) changes the most. Sections 2 and 3 are nice to have.
