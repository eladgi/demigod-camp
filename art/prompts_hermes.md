# Nano Banana prompts: Hermes Express

Save in `art\raw\` with these names. Same style as the other games (stylized 2D cartoon, bold outlines, cel shading).
The village is seen **from above at a slight angle (3/4 top-down)**, like the Labyrinth.

---

## 1. `hermes_grid.jfif`, 1:1: a 4x4 grid of the village
> A 4x4 grid sprite sheet of 16 separate objects for a top-down (3/4 view, seen from slightly above) 2D adventure game set in a small Greek village, in a stylized 2D cartoon game art style with bold clean dark outlines, cel shading and rich colors, for kids. Each item is centered in its own equal square cell with empty space around it, nothing crossing into another cell. Row 1: a small white Greek house with a terracotta roof and ONE blue-framed window, an empty doorway in the middle of the front wall with NO door in it; the same house with TWO windows; the same house with THREE windows; a plain PURE WHITE wooden door on its own (no color at all, it will be painted in the game). Row 2: a plain PURE WHITE hat with a brim on its own (no color); a girl villager in a simple Greek tunic; a boy villager; a man villager. Row 3: a woman villager; an old grandpa villager with a white beard and a walking stick; an old grandma villager with grey hair in a bun; a round stone fountain with water. Row 4: a small bed of colorful flowers; a friendly portrait of Hermes, the messenger god, head and shoulders, with a winged helmet and a big smile; a brown wrapped package tied with string; a rolled letter / scroll with a red seal. All villagers stand facing the viewer, full body, NO hats. Plain solid bright green background (#00FF00), no grid lines, no text, no shadows on the background.

## 3. `hermes_eris.jfif`, 1:1: Eris (the boss)
> A single full-body character for a top-down (3/4 view) 2D kids adventure game, stylized 2D cartoon game art style with bold clean dark outlines, cel shading and rich colors: Eris, the mischievous Greek goddess of chaos, as a playful troublemaker (not scary), a young woman with wild dark purple hair, a purple and black Greek dress, a sly grin, holding a shiny golden apple, standing facing the viewer. Plain solid bright green background (#00FF00), no text, no shadow on the background.

## 4. `menu_hermes.jfif`, 16:9: camp menu picture
> A stylized 2D cartoon game cover illustration, bold clean outlines, cel shading, rich colors: a brave young girl hero (white Greek tunic, dark braid) with little wings on her sandals flying over a sunny Greek village with white houses and colorful doors, carrying a bag full of packages and letters, Hermes with his winged helmet waving next to her, letters flying in the wind. Wide composition. No text.
